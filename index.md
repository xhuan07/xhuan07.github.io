## About Me

I am a Beckman Institute Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, working with Prof. Ying Diao. I develop microscale 3D printing methods for functional polymers and study how printing conditions control the structures that form during printing. My current work combines direct ink writing, in situ sensing, and lab automation to program structural color in bottlebrush block copolymers and the motion of soft microactuators.

I received my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from The University of Hong Kong in 2023, advised by Prof. Ji Tae Kim, and my B.Eng. in Mechatronic Engineering from Zhejiang University in 2019.

## Research Interests

- Closed-loop additive manufacturing with in situ sensing and dynamic control
- Non-equilibrium assembly during printing, including flow, evaporation, and structure formation
- Physics- and data-driven modeling of process, structure, and property relationships
- Printing-programmable stimuli-responsive architectures for soft actuators and structural color
