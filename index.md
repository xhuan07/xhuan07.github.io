## Research Interests

- Closed-loop additive manufacturing with in situ sensing and dynamic control
- Non-equilibrium assembly during printing: flow, evaporation, and structure formation
- Physics- and data-driven modeling of process–structure–property relationships
- Printing-programmable stimuli-responsive architectures for soft actuators and structural color

## Research Experience

**Beckman Institute Postdoctoral Fellow** · Jul 2024–Present  
Beckman Institute · Urbana, Illinois  
University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign  
Advisor: Prof. Ying Diao  
Co-advisors: Prof. Charles Sing, Prof. Damien Guironnet, Prof. Simon Rogers

- Developed an additive manufacturing method for bottlebrush block copolymers with programmable structural color and identified a flow-induced lamellar-to-cylinder phase transition.
- Built an automated characterization platform integrating a motorized stage, digital multimeter, and camera through a SiLA 2 server for real-time in situ electrical and optical measurements.
- Built the mechanical setup for a direct ink writing platform designed to integrate in situ characterization and Bayesian optimization for closed-loop control of structural color.
- Performed COMSOL simulations of meniscus flow and solvent evaporation to link processing conditions to structure formation.
